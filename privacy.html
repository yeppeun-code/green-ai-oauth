GreenAI Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Purpose

GreenAI uses Google Drive to store and back up research data, including satellite imagery and metadata.

Google Drive Access

The application requests Google Drive access for authorised research file transfers and backup verification.

Data Storage and Security

Research files and authentication credentials are managed in access-controlled environments.

Data Sharing and Retention

GreenAI is a three-year research project conducted by the Urban Analytics Lab. Research data are stored and managed for authorised academic research purposes. Data retention and sharing are governed by applicable institutional policies and research data management requirements.

Revoking Access

Users can revoke the application's Google account access through Google Account security settings.
